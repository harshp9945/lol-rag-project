# Champion Balance Findings (EXAMPLE — replace with real capstone output)

Hypothesis testing was applied across all champions in the dataset with
Benjamini-Hochberg correction to control the false discovery rate across
the family of tests.

A small set of champions showed win rates deviating from 50% with
statistical significance after correction, indicating balance outliers
rather than random variation.

Win-rate deviations were analysed by rank tier, since champion
performance often differs between low-rank and high-rank play due to
differences in mechanical execution and team coordination.
