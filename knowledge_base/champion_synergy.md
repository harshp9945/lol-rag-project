# Champion Synergy and Team Composition

Champion pair synergies were measured across the 20 most-picked champions,
tracking win rate when two champions appear on the same team (pairs with
at least 50 games).

The strongest synergies cluster around enchanter supports. The best pairs
are Janna with Vayne (59.6%), Twitch with Janna (59.5%), and Tristana with
Janna (58.8%); Janna appears in most of the top pairings, consistent with
her being the single strongest champion in the balance analysis. The
weakest pairs sit around 36 to 43%, for example Blitzcrank with Thresh
(35.7%), which is a double-support pairing that leaves a role gap.

These pairings tell a balance team which combinations perform above their
individual champions' win rates, separating genuine kit synergy from
coincidence.
