# Snowball Effect and Comeback Rates

Once a team falls behind in towers, comebacks are rare: the team behind in
tower kills goes on to win only about 2.1% of matches, while the team
ahead holds the lead and wins about 97.7% of the time. League at this
level is strongly snowball-driven once structural objectives start
falling.

The win probability curve by tower-kill advantage is steep. At level: a
team is at roughly 50%. One tower ahead: about 74%. Two ahead: about 90%.
Three ahead: about 95%. From four or more towers ahead, win probability is
above 98% and effectively certain.

Being behind is not an instant loss at small deficits, one tower down
still wins 23% of the time, but the curve collapses quickly, which is a
game-health point worth monitoring: it means early-game mistakes carry
heavy weight.
