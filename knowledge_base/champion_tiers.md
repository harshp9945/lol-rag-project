# Ban and Pick Meta, and Hidden Outliers

Champion threat was scored with a composite metric combining win rate
deviation from 50%, ban rate, and statistical significance, to separate
genuine threats from community perception.

The clearest finding is a set of "stealth OP" champions: statistically
significant high win rates paired with very low ban rates, meaning the
community is underrating them. The strongest examples are Sona (54.2% win
rate, 1.2% ban rate, over 5,400 games), Yorick (54.0% win, 1.0% ban),
Rammus (53.9% win, 3.6% ban), Anivia (53.6% win, 1.7% ban), and Shyvana
(53.0% win, 0.8% ban). These are exactly the champions an analyst should
flag to a balance team before the community discovers them.

At the other end, some champions carry high ban rates despite average win
rates, representing wasted community bans on perceived rather than real
threats.
