# Objective Strategy and Combinations

Objective combinations compound. The strongest three-objective
combination is Tower, Baron, and Dragon together at an 89.6% win rate.
Baron on its own is the single most valuable objective at 81.2%, well
ahead of Tower alone (70.8%), Rift Herald alone (69.5%), Dragon alone
(68.6%), and First Blood alone (59.5%).

Baron is therefore the clear number one macro priority. Where Baron is not
available, Tower plus Dragon is a viable secondary path at about 79.7%.

A logistic regression with interaction terms adds a subtlety often gotten
wrong: the Dragon and Tower combination is genuinely synergistic (positive
interaction, +0.12), but Baron with Dragon and Baron with Tower are
slightly redundant (negative interactions), meaning Baron is already so
decisive that stacking it does not multiply the effect. First Blood, at
59.5% alone with little interaction benefit, should not be prioritised
over structural objectives.
