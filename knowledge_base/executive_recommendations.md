# Executive Recommendations

High priority: review Baron Nashor's power. Baron is the single most
decisive objective at an 81.2% win rate, climbing to 89.6% when combined
with Tower and Dragon. Recommendation: evaluate whether Baron is too
swingy and monitor the impact of any change on comeback rates.

Address the confirmed balance outliers. 46 of 138 champions differ
significantly from a 50% win rate after Benjamini-Hochberg correction (23
over, 23 under). Janna (55.5% win, 41.5% ban) is the clearest genuine
problem. Separately, flag the stealth OP group, high win rate but low ban
rate, led by Sona, Yorick, Rammus, and Anivia, before the community
discovers them.

Watch the snowball curve, not the comeback story. Teams behind in towers
win only about 2.1% of games, and win probability passes 90% at a
two-tower lead. Early-game state is highly decisive, so any change that
further rewards early leads should be treated cautiously.

No action needed on pacing over time. Game duration averaged 30.5 minutes
and showed no significant trend across the season, so the meta was stable
in this window.
