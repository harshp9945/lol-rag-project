# Season Trend and Meta Stability

The dataset spans 13 weeks of Season 9 (8 June to 6 September 2017).
Weekly Team 1 win rate stays close to 50% throughout, which is an
important data-quality check: no drift means no sampling bias across the
season.

Game duration shows no significant trend over the season (Spearman rho =
-0.31, p = 0.31), so the meta was stable and snowballing did not measurably
worsen patch over patch during this window. This matters for an analyst
whose job is to detect meta-health drift between patches and flag it early;
in this season, there was nothing to flag.
