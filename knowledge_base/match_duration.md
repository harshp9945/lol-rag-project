# Match Duration and Game Health

The average ranked game lasts 30.5 minutes (standard deviation 8.5), with
most games falling between 25 and 35 minutes. The distribution is not
normal (Kolmogorov-Smirnov test, p < 0.001, slight left skew), so
non-parametric tests were used for group comparisons.

Short games (under 25 minutes) and long games (35 minutes or more) differ
significantly in character (Mann-Whitney U, p < 0.001, large effect).
Long games contain far more objectives: long games average about 0.7 Baron
kills and 2.0 Dragon kills per team, versus roughly 0.07 Baron and 0.7
Dragon in short games, confirming that longer games are the ones that
reach contested late-game objective states.

Win rate stays near 50% across all duration buckets, so neither longer nor
shorter games systematically favour a side. The overall duration profile,
peaking around 30 minutes, is healthy for a competitive MOBA.
