LoL Analytics Assistant

Ask questions about a 51,490-match League of Legends analytics study (Season 9). Answers come only from the study's findings.

What it can answer

  • Objectives: how much Baron, inhibitors, dragons and towers affect win rate
  • Champion balance: which champions are over- or under-powered, plus "stealth OP" picks
  • Comebacks and snowballing: how often teams behind actually recover
  • The win-prediction model: how it performs and why
  • Game health: match duration and meta stability over the season

It answers strictly from the study and will politely refuse anything outside it (live match advice, other games, unrelated topics). Every answer shows the source files it drew from, so you can see it isn't making things up.

Press Enter to ask. Answers are generated locally, so the first one may take a few seconds.
